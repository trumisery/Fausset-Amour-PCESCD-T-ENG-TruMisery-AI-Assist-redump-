ABOUT THE GAME
--------------
When monsters take her sister Meriya, Corque Lans sets out for Gares
Castle to bring her home. She carries two heirlooms said to have belonged
to the Apostle: a suit of armor and a two-section staff. This side-scrolling
action game tells its story through Japanese voice performances, from the
opening narration to the words Corque exchanges with each boss.

WHAT'S IN ENGLISH
-----------------
- Timed English subtitles cover the opening narration, story cinemas,
  boss conversations, ending, and spoken Continue responses. The original
  Japanese voices, music and sound effects are preserved.
- Character names, dialogue and line breaks were reviewed in context.
  Ending captions stay on screen longer to give the final lines room
  to land.
- A thin, proportional font uses white letters with black outlines.
  The picture shows through around the letters; there is no subtitle
  bar. Captions sit inside the TV margins and move to the top in crowded
  scenes to keep the artwork visible within the PC Engine's sprite limit.
- Playtesting led to fixes for text briefly reappearing after scene cuts,
  stray character graphics when captions cleared, one-frame screen jumps
  and a graphics glitch in the ending. Battle subtitles keep their white
  lettering and black outlines until they clear.
- The original debug menu has English labels. The Japanese Continue
  artwork was kept by choice; its voice responses have English captions.

ADDED CHEAT - INFINITE LIVES AND DAMAGE PROTECTION
--------------------------------------------------
Enter the code at the start of EVERY level. Press RUN to pause, then tap:

  Up, Up, Up, Down, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right

Release the direction between taps. A cheerful "Yatta!" confirms the
code. Press RUN again to resume. Corque can still lose her armor on the
first hit. Once she is unarmored, further enemy hits do no damage, and
her remaining lives do not decrease. Falls can still restart a section.
Enter the code again when you reach the next level.

ORIGINAL DEBUG MENU
-------------------
At the title screen, before starting the game, tap:

  Up, Up, Up, Down, Down, Down, II, II, II, I, I, I

Use the controller buttons marked II and I, releasing between presses.
The menu opens automatically. Use the directional pad to select a stage,
cinema, ending or staff roll, then press I to launch it. The menu also
includes CD, PSG and PCM sound tests. This code was in the Japanese game.
